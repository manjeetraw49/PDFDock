Frequently Asked Questions

Is signing PDFs on PDFDock legally valid?

Yes, electronic signatures created on PDF documents are widely recognized for contracts, agreements, and standard business forms.

Is my signature saved or stored on any server?

Never. Your signature graphic is applied locally in your browser memory and disappears as soon as you close the page.

Can I draw my signature on my phone or tablet?

Yes, the signature canvas fully supports touchscreen drawing with smooth finger and stylus input on mobile devices.
