Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stamp diagonal watermarks like 'CONFIDENTIAL' or 'DRAFT'?

Yes! Use one of our quick presets or type your own text, adjust the 45-degree angle, opacity, font size, and color with live visual preview.

Can I add dynamic page numbers?

Yes, enter 'Page {n} of {total}' into the text field to automatically stamp accurate page numbers across all pages.

Can I place text underneath or on top of document content?

Yes, you can choose 'Overlay' (stamps over content) or 'Underlay' (watermark sits beneath document text).
